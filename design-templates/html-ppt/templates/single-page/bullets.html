Why

A great deck system does three things for you

It's not that you can't make a beautiful slide. It's that you have to make one from scratch every time.

  • ① A unified design language

    Every slide shares the same tokens, so type, color, and shadow stay consistent.

  • ② Lower cost of reuse

    Next time you give a similar talk, copy the deck, swap the data, switch the theme.

  • ③ Switchable on stage

    Press T to cycle themes, A to swap animations — bonus points during the live demo.
