💡 Here's the truth

It's not how long you sleep,
it's when you wake up.

The body cycles every 90 minutes.
If your alarm yanks you out of "deep sleep",
nine hours feels like nothing.

Key idea: wake on the cycle.

Back-calculate your bedtime

👉 Formula

wake time − 90min × N − 15min to fall asleep
= when to be in bed tonight

Example: target wake-up 7am

→ 23:15 in bed (4 cycles)
→ 00:45 in bed (3 cycles)
