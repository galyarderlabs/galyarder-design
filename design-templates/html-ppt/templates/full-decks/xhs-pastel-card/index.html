Four little escapes
03 · 08

Four small things
you can hand off to AI

01

"Got it" emails

They don't need your thinking. Let AI handle them in your voice — saves about 40 minutes a week.

02

Booking, rescheduling, looking up routes

One sentence, outsourced. You only pick the final option — you don't flip through ten apps.

03

Turning meeting recordings into action items

Recording → summary → todos in one shot. You only confirm and sign.

04

Sorting last week's 300 photos

Cluster by event, pick 10 favorites, draft captions. Photo archiving is finally automated.

My auto-reply prompt
05 · 08

A single prompt
that automates "got-it" emails

# auto-reply skill
when email matches "got it / ok / confirmed / received, thanks":
  reply:
    tone: "warm, brief, not too corporate"
    max_lines: 2
    sign_with: "— Lewis"

always_skip:
  - from: ["family", "partner", "close friends"]
  - contains: ["urgent", "contract", "payment"]
