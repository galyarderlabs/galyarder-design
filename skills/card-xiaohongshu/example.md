# 5 small habits that make your AI editor feel smoother

## Cover
Save this · Must-read for AI tool fans

## Card 1: Drop the perfect-prompt mindset
Your prompt won't be perfect on the first try. Treat it as **conversation**, not SQL.

## Card 2: Show the AI your format
Before you paste Markdown or CSV, tell it "I want the output to look like X."
One concrete reference beats 50 adjectives.

## Card 3: Use templates, don't rewrite every time
Save the prompts that work as templates. HTML Anything lets you pick a template directly,
with nine world-class design systems ready to go.

## Card 4: Run a diff for follow-up edits
Regenerating is not starting from zero. After tweaking the content, send only the diff,
and let the AI fine-tune the existing HTML. You save about 80% of the tokens and keep the design style.

## Card 5: Reuse your local session
No API key needed. Let your local Claude, Cursor, or Codex run.
Zero marginal cost, and noticeably faster than a remote API.

## Wrap-up
More tools is not better. Smooth flow is what actually feels good.
